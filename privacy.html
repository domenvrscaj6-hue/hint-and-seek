Privacy, plainly

Hint & Seek exists to keep your wishes private. Here is exactly what we store, why, and for how long.

What we store

  • When you give hints: your name, your email, your recipients' emails, the occasion, your special note, your wishes and the hints you approved.
  • When you ask someone for hints: your name, your email, their email and the occasion.
  • When someone opts out: only their email address, so we never write to them again.
  • Anonymous usage counts: which pages are opened and how many hints get edited — no cookies, no IP addresses, no emails, no names and no text.

Why

  • Your email: to send you the confirmation link. Nothing reaches your recipients before you click it.
  • Recipients' emails: only to deliver the hints (or the invite) and to respect opt-outs.
  • Your wishes: only to turn them into hints. Your recipients never see the exact words you wrote in “Hints & Surprises” — only the hints you approved.

We never sell data, never show ads and never add anyone to a mailing list.

How long

  • Your original wishes are erased within a day after the hints are sent (or the confirmation link expires).
  • Unconfirmed submissions are deleted once their 48-hour link expires.
  • Sent hints and invite requests are deleted after 90 days.
  • Anonymous usage counts are deleted after 13 months.
  • Opt-outs are kept for as long as the service runs — that's how we keep the promise.

Who helps us run it

  • Google (Gemini API) rewrites the wishes from “Hints & Surprises” into hints. It receives the wish text only — never names or email addresses. We use Google's paid API, so Google does not use this text to train or improve its models.
  • Supabase stores the data listed above.
  • Resend delivers the emails.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser connects to Google when the page loads.

Cookies

None. The site sets no cookies and uses no tracking scripts.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has a one-click link to never receive Hint & Seek emails again.
  • If you'd like your data removed sooner, or have a question, write to us with the 💬 button on the main page.
